Good to know

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your repair.

Will I know the price before the repair?

Yes. After diagnosing the problem, your technician gives you a clear, upfront price. Work only starts once you approve it, and we never recommend repairs you don't need.

Can you come the same day?

Yes. We offer same-day service on most calls placed during business hours: Monday to Friday 8 AM to 6 PM. Book online after hours and we'll call you first thing the next business day.

Is there a warranty on repairs?

Every repair is backed by our 180-day guarantee. If the same problem returns within that time, we come back and make it right.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve the Sacramento region, including Fair Oaks, Carmichael, Sacramento, Roseville, Folsom, Elk Grove and Davis, and the East Bay, including Oakland, Berkeley, Richmond, Walnut Creek, Concord, Danville and San Ramon. See the full list on the service-area map.

Which brands do you repair?

All major brands, from Samsung, LG, Whirlpool, GE, Bosch and KitchenAid to premium Sub-Zero, Wolf, Thermador, Viking and Dacor. Don't see yours? Just ask, we most likely repair it too.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept credit and debit cards, Zelle, checks and cash. Military and seniors get a discount of 15% or more.
