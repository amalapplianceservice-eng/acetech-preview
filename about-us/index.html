Our story

From Global Brands to Your Kitchen

Luis Fonte Galindo spent 11 years in service roles at multinational companies, rising from technician to department head. He saw how hard it was to find appliance repair you could actually trust, and started AceTech to fix that.

Today Luis still works in the field next to our technicians, taking on the hardest diagnostics himself. Our mission is simple: professional, reliable repairs that keep your appliances running at their best, with as little disruption to your day as possible.
