Open position

Appliance Repair Technician

  • Full-time
  • On the road
  • Sacramento region & East Bay

What you'll do

  • Diagnose and repair refrigerators, washers, dryers, dishwashers, ranges, ovens and microwaves
  • Explain the problem and an upfront price to customers in plain language
  • Keep your van stocked with the parts most repairs need
  • Leave every home clean and every customer confident in the fix

What we're looking for

  • Hands-on appliance repair experience (or strong related technical experience)
  • Reliable vehicle and a valid driver's license
  • Authorization to work in the United States
  • Conversational English
  • EPA 608 certification and your own tools are a plus
