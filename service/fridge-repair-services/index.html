Refrigerator Problems We Fix

Recognize the symptom? Our technicians see it every day and carry the parts to fix it.

01

Not cooling

Warm fresh-food section, a fridge that runs but never gets cold, or temperatures that drift.

02

Freezer cold, fridge warm

Usually airflow: a failed fan, a blocked damper or a frost-clogged evaporator.

03

Leaking water

Puddles under or inside the fridge from a clogged drain, cracked line or bad inlet valve.

04

Ice maker not working

No ice, small cubes, overflowing or jammed ice makers and dispensers.

05

Water dispenser issues

No water, slow flow or a dispenser that drips after you let go.

06

Noisy or loud

Buzzing, clicking, grinding or rattling from fans, the compressor or the ice maker.

07

Runs constantly

A compressor that never shuts off and drives up your power bill.

08

Not defrosting

Frost and ice building up on the back wall or around the freezer vents.

Something else going on? Tell us and we'll take a look.

Refrigerator Repair FAQ

Quick answers before you book.

Can you repair my refrigerator the same day?

Yes. On most calls during business hours we come the same day, and our vans carry the parts most fridge repairs need, so many are finished on the first visit.

Which refrigerator brands do you repair?

All major brands: Samsung, LG, Whirlpool, GE and GE Monogram, KitchenAid, Frigidaire, Bosch and Maytag, plus built-in and premium brands like Sub-Zero, Thermador, Viking and JennAir.

Is it worth repairing my fridge or should I replace it?

Most refrigerator problems (fans, thermostats, defrost parts, valves, ice makers) cost far less to fix than a new fridge. Your technician gives you an upfront price so you can decide before any work starts.

Is the repair guaranteed?

Yes. Every refrigerator repair is backed by our 180-day guarantee. If the same issue comes back, so do we.

What should I do while I wait for the technician?

Keep the doors closed as much as possible, move perishables to a cooler with ice, and if you see water on the floor, turn off the water supply line behind the fridge.
